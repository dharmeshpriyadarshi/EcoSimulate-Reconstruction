# ECOSIMULATE Experimental Analysis

## Analytical scope

Regional analysis describes applied hypothetical scenarios
within the currently active geographical study region.

Unconfigured cells are excluded from pollution statistics.
They are not interpreted as zero-pollution environments.

## Descriptive statistics

The analysis reports arithmetic means, minimum and maximum
values, and population standard deviations.

The current spatial framework uses equal nominal cell
areas within each active geographical region.

Arithmetic means therefore describe the configured cells
without unequal-area weighting.

These descriptive statistics do not establish confidence
intervals, statistical significance or population inference.

## Regional relative reduction

Relative reduction is calculated from the difference
between regional mean baseline and intervention values,
divided by the regional mean baseline.

It is not the arithmetic mean of individual percentage
reductions.

Relative reduction is undefined when the mean baseline
equals zero.

## Deployment density

Total hypothetical deployment is the sum of artificial-tree
counts across configured cells.

Deployment density divides this total by the configured
geographical area in hectares.

Unconfigured area is excluded from this density calculation.

## Sensitivity analysis

Deployment sensitivity is calculated by varying artificial-tree
counts while holding other applied scenario parameters constant.

The sensitivity engine reuses the existing mathematical models.

The sampled deployment range includes zero, the applied
deployment count and the maximum supported count.

Sensitivity curves describe mathematical model behaviour.
They do not constitute independently validated dose-response
relationships or physical effectiveness estimates.

## Model separation

The published custom Pollution Index is dimensionless and
reported in index points.

The illustrative PM2.5 model reports hypothetical concentration
values in micrograms per cubic metre.

Outputs from these models must not be combined into a
single regional statistical distribution.

## Scientific limitations

No atmospheric transport, cross-cell dispersion, measured
intervention effectiveness, uncertainty propagation or
causal identification is represented.

The analysis is deterministic and intended for transparent
exploration of hypothetical environmental scenarios.
